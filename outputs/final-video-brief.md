1 coaching idea goes into a 4-agent Claude Code relay, and 13 minutes later it comes out as a written offer, a lead magnet and a 5-email follow-up. Then the viewer gets the exact files.

## Who it's for

Solo coaches and consultants who sell a high-ticket service by conversation. They have a good offer in their head, and nothing written behind it: no clear offer page, no lead magnet, no follow-up. They've tried AI tools and got generic copy they couldn't use. They don't need another tool. They need to know what to build first.

Everyone else in the brief (creators, service providers, entrepreneurs) can watch and use it. The copy speaks to this buyer.

## The pain, and why it costs them now

Their offer lives only in their head. Every prospect hears a slightly different version, every discovery call starts from zero, and nothing works for them between calls. Each week without a written offer and a follow-up is another week of re-explaining it by hand to people who then go quiet. A chatbot doesn't fix this, because nothing it writes connects to anything else. (Inference: drawn from the brief's "overwhelmed, don't know what to build first," not measured data.)

**The line to say on camera:** "You don't have a tools problem. You have nothing working for you between calls."

## The promise

1 idea in, 5 connected assets out, in 1 working session: positioning, an offer, YouTube angles, a lead magnet and a 5-email follow-up. **It produces the assets, not the sales.** No income is promised or implied.

## The mechanism: the Offer Relay

4 specialist agents run in a fixed order, and each one can only work from the file the previous one wrote.

```
market-signal-researcher -> offer-architect -> [YOU EDIT 1 LINE] -> content-angle-strategist -> conversion-system-builder
```

- **The researcher is read-only.** It scores demand and can't skip ahead to writing copy.
- **Every handoff is a file.** You can open it, change it and re-run from there.
- **1 edit flows downstream.** Change 1 line of the offer and the titles and emails inherit it. That is the moment that makes it a system instead of a prompt.
- **Weak output is traceable.** It leads back to a weak line in the brief, not a mystery.

**The thesis, said once and shown the whole time:** the money is not in tools. It is in systems. A prompt gives you an answer. A relay gives you a process you control.
