### Best Title
**One Idea In. A Full Client-Getting System Out. (Claude Code)**

Why it wins:
- Understood in 3 seconds: the outcome (a system that gets clients) is the headline. The tool is secondary, as the signal brief recommends.
- Uses the "system that creates [business outcome]" pattern and speaks to the coach/consultant's existing desire for a lead magnet and follow-up.
- "Claude Code" sits in parentheses so developer-leaning wording does not scare off non-technical viewers. (Inference)
- "Idea in, system out" promises something the video can literally show: one input, four files, one finished stack.
