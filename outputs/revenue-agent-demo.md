## 3. Why the topic should work

- **The pain is sequencing, not awareness.** The audience knows AI matters and is stuck on what to build first. A fixed chain answers that question for them.
- **The output is a checklist they already want.** Positioning, offer, lead magnet and follow-up are the standard coach launch stack. (Inference)

## 5. The unique mechanism

**The Revenue Agent Chain** (working name). 4 specialist agents, run in order by a coordinator:

1. **market-signal-researcher**: scores demand and buyer psychology. Read-only, so it can't skip ahead to writing.
2. **offer-architect**: turns the signal into positioning, an offer and a value ladder.
3. *Human review gate*: the creator edits 1 line of the offer before anything downstream runs.
4. **content-angle-strategist**: titles, hook, retention map.
5. **conversion-system-builder**: lead magnet, CTA, 5-email follow-up.

**Why it's believable:** every handoff is a file you can open, edit and re-run. A one-off prompt gives you 1 answer. A chain gives you an inspectable process that improves when you sharpen the input.

## 10. What the creator must show on screen to be credible

- A **live, unedited run** on 1 specific niche, with each output file appearing in the `outputs` folder.
- **Handoffs:** each agent visibly reading the previous file.
- **1 human edit** with before and after, plus its effect downstream.
- The **real folder structure**, briefly, so the template is obviously real.
- The **"(Inference)" labels**, pointed out on purpose. Admitting what's a guess builds trust.
- An honest line: **"This produces the assets, not the sales."**
- **Nothing implied about income.** No past-client results exist in these materials, so none are claimed.

## 11. What to remove because it feels generic

- Any tool roundup, "top 10 AI tools," or comparing Claude to other chatbots.
- Prompt-writing tips. This is a systems video, not a prompt lesson.
- More than 2 minutes on setup or configuration.
- "AI will save you hours" and any time-saving framing without a revenue link.
- A vague demo idea like "a coaching business."
- Hype words and income screenshots that can't be backed up.
- A 5-agent diagram. The real system has 4 agents, and the video must match the template.
